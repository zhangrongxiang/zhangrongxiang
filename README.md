##  ɪ'ᴍ Jack Zhang

*人，可以生如蚁而美如神 — 顾城*

I am an undergraduate student at **Harbin Institute of Technology (Weihai)** and an incoming PhD student at **School of Artificial Intelligence, Shanghai Jiao Tong University** (through joint program with **Shanghai Innovation Institute**), advised by [Prof.Songhua Liu](https://huage001.github.io/). 
Prior to that, I  was a remote student of LINS LAB, directed by [Prof.Tao Lin](https://tlin-taolin.github.io/).
I have also done a research internship in [Wayne Xin Zhao](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=JNhNacoAAAAJ&hl=en) 's AI BOX group(Gaoling School,Renmin University of China).

My research interests include **generative models,computer vision,  and machine learning theory.** I am dedicated to building efficient generative model systems and exploring the application of generative models in computer vision.
